OpenDose data shipped with radot: reference organ masses (masses.json, g) and Lu177 S-values
(lu177.json.gz, mGy/(MBq.s)) of the ICRP 110 adult male and female phantoms. The other
radionuclides are downloaded on first use into the radot cache (see docs/5b_opendose.md).

Source: OpenDose collaboration, https://opendose.org. Please cite: Chauvin M. et al., "OpenDose:
open-access resource for nuclear medicine dosimetry", J Nucl Med 2020, doi:10.2967/jnumed.119.240366.
